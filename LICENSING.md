# Website licensing and provenance policy

This source repository is private and proprietary; see `LICENSE`. A public-facing deployment does not publish or open-source its source. Third-party packages remain under their upstream licenses and deployed notices have a stable public path at `/legal/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt`.

First-party project images, badges, logos, and distinctive presentation assets are private project assets with branding rights reserved. AI-generated assets are disclosed in the private Pass-III provenance matrix where local C2PA or canonical history establishes that fact; no asset is falsely attributed to a human author. The canonical Flower-of-Life PNG was recovered from the private information asset library and restored byte-for-byte, with exact provenance and visual evidence retained privately.

Generated files, dependency locks, Workers bindings, and schema-derived validators preserve their generation provenance and do not imply first-party authorship of upstream formats or packages.

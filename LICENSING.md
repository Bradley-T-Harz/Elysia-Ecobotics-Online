# Website licensing and provenance policy

This source repository is private and proprietary; see `LICENSE`. A public-facing deployment does not publish or open-source its source. Third-party packages remain under their upstream licenses and deployed notices have a stable public path at `/legal/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt`. Media provenance and current rights dispositions are recorded in `ASSET_NOTICES.md` and at `/legal/third-party-media-credits`.

First-party project images, badges, logos, and distinctive presentation assets are private project assets with branding rights reserved. AI-generated assets are disclosed in the private Pass-III provenance matrix where local C2PA or canonical history establishes that fact; no asset is falsely attributed to a human author. The Flower-of-Life PNG is separately identified in `ASSET_NOTICES.md`; its public attribution does not substitute for the still-image license or permission evidence that remains pending.

Generated files, dependency locks, Workers bindings, and schema-derived validators preserve their generation provenance and do not imply first-party authorship of upstream formats or packages.

# First-party lanes

| Lane | Gate | Activation dependency |
|---|---|---|
| One-time Support | OFF | Separate sandbox, restricted keys, webhook/receipt acceptance, tax/legal/preflight |
| Recurring Support | OFF | Above plus recurring invoices, Portal cancellation and failed-renewal acceptance |
| Commercial Job Post | OFF | Above plus canonical 1000-cent fee, governed waiver/reduction and publication separation acceptance |
| Organization/professional service | OFF | Existing approved agreement and authorized payer; current production has none |
| Governed sponsorship | OFF | Existing approved agreement and authorized payer; current production has none |

No agreement, offer or tax conclusion was invented. Support grants no governance/status/priority/personal compute. Payment cannot buy Job Post publication or ranking. See [canonical lane implementation](../stripe-first-party-2026-09-15/FIRST_PARTY_PAYMENT_LANES.md) and [exact runbook](STRIPE_DASHBOARD_HUMAN_RUNBOOK.md).

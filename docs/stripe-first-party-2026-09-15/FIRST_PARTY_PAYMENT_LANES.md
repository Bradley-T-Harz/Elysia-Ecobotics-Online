# First-party lanes

| Order | Lane | Economic authority | Current state |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | One-time Support | Validated supporter choice within canonical USD limits; current consent; no privileges | OFF |
| 2 | Monthly Support | Server-owned $1/$5/$12/$25/$50 price codes; explicit monthly choice; linked recoverable account | OFF |
| 3 | Commercial Job Post | Reviewed economic condition; standard 1000 USD cents; governed waiver or 50–999 cent reduction | OFF |
| 4 | Organization/professional service | Existing approved service/agreement and authorized billing member; amount/currency from owned record | OFF; no new service invented |
| 5 | Governed sponsorship | Existing approved agreement and payer; separate recognition preference | OFF; no new sponsorship invented |

Support buys no governance, status, review, visibility, access or personal compute. Monthly cancellation is at period end in the prepared Portal configuration. Renewal amount is checked against the canonical price. No monthly option is preselected.

Job Post payment closes only its economic condition; content review, publication and ranking remain separate. Free, exempt and fully waived paths need no Stripe session. A reduction requires both fee-assessment and assistance capabilities and an operator different from the author. Payment does not cure rejection. EcoSyneva failure to publish after payment follows governed refund review.

Services use the existing payment/invoice reference workflow. This program does not invent a separate Stripe Invoicing product or an open-ended customer-selected service price. Accounting export retains order/payment/refund/dispute references, gross, provider-confirmed fee/net and source event evidence.

Production inventory at verification: zero organizations, zero service engagements and zero sponsorship agreements. Three existing Job Post economic conditions were preserved. No service/sponsorship offer or price was invented.

# Creator Studio and Forge

Creator Studio is a first-class Marketplace navigation destination at `/marketplace/creator-studio`. Forge links to the Studio, and the Studio links back to Forge, a new draft, Marketplace Account and Signals. Existing account/publisher/ownership surfaces remain authoritative; there is no duplicate application.

The Studio explains free creation/review/publication, the adopted 5% paid commission (inactive), zero platform commission for free listings, and disabled paid seller onboarding/payouts. Developer identity, publisher authority, seller state and public account role remain distinct. Public visitors can reach documentation and creation entrances without a payments runtime. Signed-in owned records still require their original authorization.

Navigation contract and focused browser checks cover canonical links and mobile layout. Existing September publisher ownership evidence covers unchanged ownership behavior; no broad historical audit was restarted.

# Ecological subsystem concepts

These seven concepts are preserved for future Earth System AI architecture, ecological/environmental domain specialization, and a possible associated Elysia add-on. They are conceptual directions, not claims that seven implemented subsystems currently define the complete Elysia product.

The descriptions below were formerly presented as homepage cards. Removing that presentation broadens the homepage's positioning; it does not repudiate or retire these concepts. Existing Story, research-note, project history and architectural references remain valid in their own context.

| Concept | Preserved description |
|---|---|
| Verdante | Plants, gardens, restoration, and practical green care. |
| Sylphora | Animals, wildlife, pollinators, care, and interspecies understanding. |
| Ecotiva | Ecosystems, habitats, interdependence, and the living weave of place. |
| Aurania | Air, atmosphere, climate, weather, and the moving patterns above us. |
| Terraflux | Soil, land, terrain, hazards, and changing ground conditions. |
| Aquaria | Water, wetlands, watersheds, streams, and careful monitoring. |
| Aetheria | Space, planetary awareness, wider systems, and the long horizon of intelligence. |

## Positioning and truth standard

Ecology is a core domain and moral concern of EcoSyneva, not a limitation on who Elysia is for.

Poetic language may shape the experience. Factual language must describe the system. Environmental benefit, restoration, sustainability, carbon benefit, conservation impact and other ecological outcomes require supporting evidence. Imagery, names, metaphors and aesthetics do not themselves establish impact.

Internal formulation: ecologically grounded, broadly useful, and unwilling to pretend that aesthetic greenness is environmental impact. This does not prescribe extra public marketing copy or authorize an Earth System model/add-on implementation.
